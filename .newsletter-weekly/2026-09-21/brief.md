#### AX: an execution platform for agentic tasks
Source: AX · Category: Cloud
- AX presents a declarative platform for running isolated, stateful agent tasks at very large scale.
- Its four primitives are Task, Workspace, Gateway, and Model; they cover execution, setup, network policy, and model configuration.
- The product emphasizes suspend-and-resume behavior, dense multiplexing, reproducible workspaces, and research workloads such as reinforcement learning and evaluations.

#### ChatGPT now knows what you do on other websites via ad collector
Source: Buchodi's Threat Intel · Category: Security
- The report says ChatGPT creates a cross-site __obi cookie on .openai.com that advertiser pages can send to OpenAI when they load its advertising SDK.
- The author observed the identifier on requests from commercial sites and reports that the SDK can collect hashed contact fields, location fields, page text, and page paths.
- These are independently unverified observations; the report says OpenAI acknowledged the inquiry but did not answer its questions before publication.

#### GPT-6 Astra: 3D, Embodied AI, and Beyond
Source: X · Author: Wentao Zhu · Category: AI
- Wentao Zhu argues that GPT-6 Astra's visible 3D capability is an agentic inverse-graphics loop: write code, render, inspect, revise, and repeat.
- The article frames a stronger foundation model as an orchestrator for robotics tools and as a generator of demonstrations that can be distilled into fast on-device policies.
- It separates model problems from infrastructure limits, highlighting simulator fidelity, time-aware physical reasoning, sensing, and high-degree-of-freedom control.

### 2026-09-22

#### I Don’t Want to Read What You Didn’t Write
Source: Colin Breck · Author: Colin Breck · Category: AI
- Colin Breck argues that AI-generated workplace prose often removes the context, judgment, and human voice that make writing worth reading.
- The essay cites a survey in which 78% of readers would stop reading AI-assisted or AI-authored articles, while 98% preferred an author's own imperfect writing.
- Breck finds AI most useful as a verifier, editor, citation assistant, and diagramming tool—not as the primary author of substantive prose.

#### Humanoid robot sales tally hit 7,000 globally last year
Source: Reuters / The Economic Times · Category: Business
- The International Federation of Robotics (IFR) counted about 7,000 humanoid robots sold worldwide in 2025 for industrial and professional-service use.
- That remains tiny beside 542,000 industrial robots installed and an estimated 199,000 service robots sold in 2024; many humanoids were acquired for research or AI-data generation rather than productive work.
- Bank of America Global Research forecasts 90,000 humanoid shipments in 2026 and 1.2 million by 2030, a sharply more optimistic trajectory than the current installed base suggests.

### 2026-09-23

#### The current balance of power in open models
Source: Interconnects AI · Author: Nathan Lambert · Category: AI
- Lambert argues Chinese labs currently lead U.S. labs in open-weight model capability, downloads, and adoption, with leading Chinese models roughly 2–5 months behind the closed U.S. frontier.
- Open-model use has become economically meaningful: OpenRouter usage reportedly rose from about 1T tokens weekly in September 2025 to about 80T, while Chinese models account for over 80% of usage there.
- Lambert urges continued U.S. investment in open models and ecosystem-level risk preparation, since restricting releases could also disadvantage U.S. businesses and researchers.

#### Humanoid robot sales tally hit 7,000 globally last year
출처: 로이터(Reuters) / 이코노믹 타임스(The Economic Times) · 카테고리: 비즈니스(Business)
- 국제로봇연맹(International Federation of Robotics, IFR)은 2025년 전 세계 산업·전문 서비스 용도의 휴머노이드 로봇 약 7,000대가 판매됐다고 집계했다.

- 뱅크오브아메리카 글로벌 리서치(Bank of America Global Research)는 2026년 9만 대, 2030년 120만 대 출하를 전망했으며, 이는 현재 기반 규모보다 훨씬 낙관적인 경로다.

### 2026-09-23

#### The current balance of power in open models
출처: 인터커넥츠 AI(Interconnects AI) · 작성자: 네이선 램버트(Nathan Lambert) · 카테고리: AI
- 램버트는 중국 연구소가 개방형 가중치 모델의 성능, 다운로드, 도입률에서 미국 연구소를 앞서며, 선두 중국 모델과 미국의 비공개 최첨단 모델 간 격차는 약 2~5개월이라고 주장한다.
- 개방형 모델은 경제적으로 의미 있는 규모로 쓰이기 시작했다. 오픈라우터(OpenRouter)의 주간 이용량은 2025년 9월 약 1조 토큰에서 약 80조 토큰으로 늘었고, 그중 중국 모델 비중은 80%를 넘었다고 한다.
- 램버트는 미국 기업과 연구자도 출시 제한으로 불리해질 수 있으므로, 미국이 개방형 모델 투자를 이어가고 생태계 차원에서 위험에 대비해야 한다고 촉구한다.
