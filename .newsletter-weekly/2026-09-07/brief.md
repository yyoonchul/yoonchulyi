#### An Alien Mind
Source: OpenAI · Author: Jakub Pachocki · Category: AI
- OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki argues that scaling is producing increasingly capable systems whose general behavior remains only partly understood.
- The essay separates goal alignment from value alignment and says robust generalization of human values is the central unresolved safety problem.

#### Your intellectual fly is open
Source: The Observation Deck · Author: Bryan Cantrill · Category: DevTools
- Bryan Cantrill says obvious LLM-authored social posts damage trust because readers can no longer tell which ideas genuinely belong to the author.
- The criticism is not of all LLM use: Cantrill recommends them for brainstorming, understanding text, and editing rather than for supplying a person’s voice.
- The practical advice is simple: write the substantive message yourself, then use tools only where they preserve rather than replace authenticity.

#### AI Just Solved a 350-Year-Old Math Problem By Writing the Longest Proof Ever
Source: Decrypt · Author: Jose Antonio Lanz · Category: AI
- Anthropic says Claude formalized Fermat’s Last Theorem in Lean in 11 days, producing a 13-million-line proof that a computer can check step by step.
- The effort used dozens of agents and Prove2Me to coordinate tasks; the result includes more than 30,000 supporting theorems and consumed billions of tokens.
- This is not a new proof of the theorem, which Andrew Wiles established in 1995, but a machine-verifiable formalization that can be independently inspected on GitHub.

#### Working on Economics with Fable 5
Source: Wilsons Blog · Category: Business
- The author and a co-author at the Stockholm School of Economics propose a task-based model that pins aggregate wages to technology, machine production, and scarce inputs.
- The model combines Acemoglu–Restrepo-style marginal tasks with recursive input-output logic, yielding a wage equation driven by machine cost, human advantage, and scarcity rents.
- The authors argue that AI could depress the human edge in tasks; they propose taxing scarce assets and using a sovereign wealth fund to spread the resulting returns.

#### Humanoid robots are being prepared for combat as China moves them from labs to military training grounds
Source: TechSpot · Author: Rob Thubron · Category: Security
- China is accelerating research on military uses for humanoid robots after the PLA Daily urged technology transfer from laboratories to training grounds.
- Proposed roles include reconnaissance, logistics, patrols, tunnel and ship operations, and combined assaults with robot dogs and unmanned vehicles.
- The report finds no operational deployment of armed humanoids, and flags energy limits, falls on rough terrain, cost, and civilian-identification risks as major barriers.

#### GPT-6 Astra on robotic manipulation
Source: Robocurve · Category: AI
- In 20 trials on a block-into-bowl task, GPT-6 Astra completed 19 runs, compared with 8 for Fable 5.1 and 1 for Fable 5.
- Astra averaged 2.5 minutes and an estimated $0.94 per bowl-task run, versus Fable 5.1’s 6.8 minutes and $2.12.
- On a harder puzzle-piece insertion task, Astra completed only 2 of 20 trials—the same completion count as Fable 5.1—showing that final-stage contact manipulation remains difficult.
