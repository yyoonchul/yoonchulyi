#### How to Build a Brain in the AI Age
Source: X · Author: Nathan (@nathannn_critch) · Category: AI
- Nathan Critchett argues that people should strengthen their thinking before learning AI tools; AI amplifies existing judgment rather than supplying it.
- He distinguishes horizontal development (adding tools and knowledge) from vertical development (changing how one thinks), and recommends metacognition, judgment, and comfort with ambiguity.
- The article cites reported examples including a 24% exam-score decline among students who outsourced homework to AI, while emphasizing that unsupported use—not AI inherently—is the problem.

- TechCrunch’s Sean O’Kane found unclaimed money with Muse, but viewed the one-time success as a party trick rather than evidence of lasting usefulness.
- Muse could become more useful with access to financial and email data, but the panel questioned whether users will trust Meta with sensitive information given its ad business.

Source: TechCrunch · Category: Security

#### Meta wants your next gadget to be Muse-infused

- The company built 5,000 "Muse Home Link" USB-C dongles that connect the AI to smart home devices, giving them away for free to Muse subscribers.
- The initiative is part of Meta's broader strategy to push Muse beyond a standalone chatbot and into various hardware form factors and enterprise applications.
