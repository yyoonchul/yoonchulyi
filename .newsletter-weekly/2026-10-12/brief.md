#### Character.AI files for bankruptcy as Disney bids $20M for some assets
Source: The Next Web · Category: Business
- Character Technologies filed for Chapter 11 in Delaware and plans to sell most or all of its assets through a court-supervised process.

#### Anthropic study warns that small AI models could soon replicate across everyday devices
Source: The Decoder · Category: AI
- Anthropic researchers tested whether small models could autonomously propagate across consumer IoT devices; the experiments omitted the initial hacking step.
- GLM-5.2 completed the tested replication chain across eight nodes in four of five runs, while Qwen3.5-9B improved with an iterative strategy-sharing setup.
- No model currently combines the capability and small footprint needed for real-world spread; the researchers warn that this gap may close as models improve.

#### Meta’s new commercial terms will allow the scraping of news sites, legal experts say
Source: Nieman Lab · Category: AI
- New Meta commercial terms, effective October 30, say the company may crawl websites associated with business accounts and use collected content to develop and train its products.
- Three legal experts told Nieman Lab that the language could grant broad scraping permissions, though enforceability and its interaction with robots.txt remain unsettled.
- Businesses that reject the terms must stop using Meta products for commercial purposes; publishers face a choice between accepting the terms and leaving business accounts.

#### 앤스로픽 연구, 소형 AI 모델의 가정용 기기 간 복제 가능성 경고
출처: The Decoder · 카테고리: AI
- 앤스로픽 연구진은 소형 모델이 소비자용 사물인터넷 기기 사이에서 자율적으로 퍼질 수 있는지 실험했다. 최초 침입 단계는 실험에 포함하지 않았다.

- 현재 실제 확산에 필요한 성능과 작은 크기를 동시에 갖춘 모델은 없지만, 연구진은 모델이 발전하면서 그 간극이 줄어들 수 있다고 경고한다.

#### Meta’s new commercial terms will allow the scraping of news sites, legal experts say
출처: Nieman Lab · 카테고리: 인공지능
- 10월 30일부터 적용되는 Meta의 새 상업 약관은 비즈니스 계정과 연결된 웹사이트를 수집하고, 모은 콘텐츠를 제품 개발과 학습에 사용할 수 있다고 규정한다.
