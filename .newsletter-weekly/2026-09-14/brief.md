### 2026-09-14

#### What's behind the AI industry's latest warnings of doom?
Source: TechCrunch · Category: AI
- Recent apocalyptic AI warnings from Anthropic researchers spark debate over the industry's motivations.
- Skeptics argue that emphasizing existential danger might be a cynical strategy to inflate valuations by boasting about model capabilities.
- Fixating on the hypothetical doom of AGI distracts from immediate AI challenges like labor impacts and environmental concerns.

#### You need to be delusional if you want to succeed
Source: X · Author: @thedankoe · Category: Business
- Pursuing realistic goals often traps individuals in societal expectations and leads to unfulfilling mediocrity.
- Setting impossible, "delusional" goals forces the brain into rapid learning and filters out useless habits.
- Achieving these goals requires brutally cutting out distractions and focusing entirely on high-leverage force multipliers.

### 2026-09-15

#### Product Management is still about telling stories
Source: Josh Elman on X · Category: Product Management
- Product management is fundamentally about telling the story of the users and why the product matters in their lives, not just writing technical specs.
- AI has inverted the product development loop from "spec and scope" to "build and play," making prototypes cheap but emphasizing the need for sound PM judgment.
- Effective user onboarding is the most critical opportunity to tell your product's story and should introduce core concepts step-by-step rather than dropping users into a blank interface.

### 2026-09-16

#### Apple's Siri AI Can Be Swapped Out for Claude, ChatGPT, Code Shows
Source: MacRumors · Category: AI
- Private iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate frameworks reportedly show two paths for third-party AI: an in-app “Ask…” extension and deeper replacement of Siri’s server-side model.
- A Claude extension was demonstrated interpreting a reminder request for Siri and creating a CSV in Siri’s conversation window; the released software currently exposes only ChatGPT.
- The deeper Inference Providing protocol could pass Apple’s planner prompt and tool definitions to an external model, letting it request system actions while Siri retains the interface and voice.

#### The Physical AI Value Stack: Mapping the 11 Layers of Robotics
Source: X · Author: Michelle Sun · Category: AI
- Michelle Sun proposes a task-first framework of 11 layers, tracing a robot task from end effector through actuation, power, control, models, data, compute, simulation, and evaluation.
- The framework asks which layer limits a specific job, rather than treating the AI model as the default bottleneck.
- Sun argues that durable robotics value comes from solving the current constraint and creating a feedback loop of deployment, data, and better models.

### 2026-09-17

#### Introducing System One Models & Jev
Source: TypeSafe AI Blog · Category: AI
- TypeSafe AI introduced Jev, its first public System One Model, in early access for software automation.
- Rather than generating text token by token, Jev returns typed probabilistic decisions in parallel; the company says its end-to-end response time is 70–500 ms.
- TypeSafe reports up to 193.6× faster and 444.6× cheaper workflow results, while acknowledging that these are at the high end and its in-house workflow evaluation may contain bias.

### 2026-09-18

#### Google announces new experimental “CC” AI agent for families
Source: Ars Technica · Category: AI
- Google Labs introduced CC, an experimental shared AI agent for a family group of up to six people.
- CC can assemble a shared daily brief, manage shared calendars, prepare documents, and use explicitly shared email, Google Chat, and Google Drive content.
- The experiment runs in an isolated cloud instance with Antigravity and Gemini 3.8 Flash; it currently supports only personal Gmail accounts belonging to adults aged 18 or older.

### 2026-09-15

#### Product Management is still about telling stories
출처: Josh Elman on X · 카테고리: Product Management
- 제품 관리(Product Management)의 본질은 단순한 기술 스펙(spec) 작성이 아니라, 사용자와 제품이 그들의 삶에 왜 중요한지에 대한 이야기를 들려주는 것이다.
- AI는 제품 개발 루프를 '스펙과 범위 설정(spec and scope)'에서 '구축과 플레이(build and play)'로 역전시켰으며, 프로토타입 제작 비용을 크게 낮췄지만 제품 관리에 대한 판단력의 중요성은 더욱 커졌다.
- 효과적인 온보딩(Onboarding)은 제품의 이야기를 전달할 수 있는 가장 중요한 기회이며, 사용자에게 빈 화면을 제시하기보다는 핵심 개념을 단계별로 가르쳐야 한다.
