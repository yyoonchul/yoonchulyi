### 2. LLMs Get Lost in Evolving User Intent

1. The paper targets a gap between benchmark design and real collaboration.
   - Most LLM evaluation still gives a complete task in one turn.
   - Real users reveal information gradually, revise a requirement, or pivot to a related task.
   - The authors ask whether models can track and act on that changing intent.
   - The work is by Jihoon Tack, Philippe Laban, and Jennifer Neville at Microsoft Research.
2. The framework reuses existing verifiable datasets.
   - It anchors the source task’s intent at the final conversation turn.
   - Earlier turns are synthesized as plausible paths toward that final task.
   - The source dataset’s original verifier can still score the final answer automatically.
   - This avoids new labels or complete reliance on an LLM-as-a-judge.
3. It models three kinds of conversational change.
   - Argument reveal adds unknown information while the underlying task remains fixed.
   - Argument revision changes an already supplied value, requiring an agent to update its belief.
   - Function switching pivots to a related task while retaining applicable context.
   - The authors apply the changes across GSM8K, BIRD-SQL, search, and software-engineering tasks.
4. Performance declines as these changes accumulate.
   - Static-setting performance did not reliably transfer across model families.
   - The paper reports drops up to roughly 30% from source single-turn accuracy in some cases.
   - GPT-5.5’s math accuracy declined from 99.0% to 80.5% after six intent transitions.
   - Function switches were generally more damaging than information reveals or revisions.
5. The analysis points to mitigation directions rather than a solved problem.
   - Adding turns with no intent changes did not cause the same decline.
   - Oracle recaps improved performance but did not restore original single-turn accuracy.
   - Better per-turn reasoning produced at most small gains in the tested setting.
   - Reinforcement learning on evolving-intent examples raised Qwen3-4B from 64.0% to 76.0% while preserving single-turn performance.

   - 준비(preparation)는 자원과 부족한 점을 파악한 뒤 여유를 쌓고 피할 수 있는 위험을 줄이는 일이다.
5. 실행은 제한된 주의력과 반복적 적응에 의존한다.
   - 집중(focus)은 제한된 자원을 결정적인 병목에 모으는 것으로 설명된다.
   - 실행은 장기 비전을 연간, 90일, 주간, 일간 목표와 연결해야 한다.
   - 적응(adapt)은 핵심 의도는 지키되 상황이 바뀌면 전술을 바꾸는 일이다.
   - 글은 주간 검토, 성찰, 예측과 여러 분야에 걸친 원리 학습을 권하며 마무리한다.
